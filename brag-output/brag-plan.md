# Brag plan — Local LLM Arena

## Rubric

1. **What it is:** A local Ollama eval UI that runs one prompt against up to six models at once, streams answers in parallel, and lets you vote and track Elo — on your machine.
2. **Audience:** Developers choosing a local model without sending prompts to a cloud API.
3. **Strongest claim:** Compare local models on one prompt.
4. **Visual identity:** Forge — warm charcoal `oklch(0.178 0.008 65)`, cream foreground, single ember accent, Bricolage Grotesque + Hanken Grotesk.
5. **Must-show UI:** Empty-state headline, model chips, three ArenaCards, FAST crown on the fastest stream.
6. **Hook (2–3s):** “Chat UIs talk to one model.”
7. **Outro:** “Compare. Score. Choose.” + Local LLM Arena wordmark.
8. **Tone:** Polished (inferred). Landscape 1920×1080, 20s. No voice.
9. **Audio:** Vol-12 happy-beats bed at low gain; sparse UI SFX on card/logo beats.

## Angle

A forge, not a chatbot. One question hits three local models; the fastest earns the crown. Entirely on-device.
